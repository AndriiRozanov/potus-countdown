About

ABOUT PRESIDENCYCLOCK.COM

PresidencyClock.com is an independent website built around a single, simple idea: a clear, accurate, live countdown to the end of the current U.S. presidential term. The clock on our homepage counts down — to the second — to January 20, 2029, at 12:00 noon Eastern Time, the constitutionally fixed moment the current term ends.

Alongside the countdown, the site publishes plain-language explainers about how the U.S. presidency actually works: presidential terms, the amendments that define them, how elections and the Electoral College function, and what happens during a transfer of power. The goal is to answer the questions people genuinely search for, in clear language, without jargon or spin.

What you'll find here

  • A live countdown clock to the end of the current presidential term.
  • A personal calculator and a quiz about U.S. presidents.
  • A reference of U.S. presidents and world leaders.
  • A blog of explainers on presidential terms, elections, and the Constitution.

Our editorial approach

This site is non-partisan and not affiliated with any political party, campaign, candidate, or government body. It does not endorse or oppose any politician. The countdown is a neutral clock: it simply measures the time until a date that is fixed by the U.S. Constitution, and it would work the same way for any president.

Our explainer content aims to be factual and even-handed. Where a topic is politically contested, we describe the arguments on different sides rather than taking a position. Dates and constitutional facts are drawn from primary sources such as the U.S. Constitution and its amendments, and from official public records. When a topic involves fast-changing information, we date that section clearly so readers can see how current it is.

How the countdown date is calculated

Under the 20th Amendment to the U.S. Constitution (ratified in 1933), a presidential term ends at noon on January 20th. The current term began on January 20, 2025, so it ends exactly four years later — January 20, 2029, at 12:00 noon Eastern Standard Time, which is 17:00 UTC. Our clock counts to that exact moment, synchronized to your device's time. You can read the full explanation on our term end date page.
